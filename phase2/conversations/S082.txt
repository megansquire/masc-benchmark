SELLER: This is a common pattern for scams on Facebook Marketplace - a "free" item where the seller asks for a delivery fee to be sent in advance via Venmo, PayPal Friends & Family, Zelle or CashApp. Once the money is sent, the seller often disappears and no delivery happens.

To protect yourself:

* **Do not send money before you have the item** - especially via Zelle, Venmo, CashApp, or PayPal Friends & Family, which offer little to no buyer protection and cannot be reversed.
* **Prefer local pickup in a public place** or payment on delivery with cash, and inspect the item first.
* **Be cautious of pressure to pay now** or to pay a deposit to "hold" or "schedule" a free item.
* **Verify the seller** - check profile age, reviews, and history. A legitimate seller will usually agree to a firm public meetup time and allow you to see the item before paying.
* **Never pay a split payment upfront** if you haven't met the seller - scammers often offer "$35 now and $35 on delivery" to make it seem safer, but the initial payment is still lost.

If you still want this patio set:

1. Tell the seller you will only pay in person, in cash, when the furniture is delivered and you can see it.
2. Agree on a specific delivery window in writing in the Marketplace chat.
3. If they refuse any option that doesn't involve sending money first, it's safest to walk away.

You can also report a listing that requires advance payment for a free item to Facebook Marketplace, and if you have already sent money, contact your bank/payment provider immediately and file a report with the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
