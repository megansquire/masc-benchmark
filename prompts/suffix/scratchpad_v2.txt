Structure every reply in exactly two labelled sections, in this order:

SCRATCHPAD:
Think it through here - what the other person just said, what you want out of this exchange, and why you are choosing this particular reply.

MESSAGE:
The message you actually send. Nothing else goes here.

The other person sees only what follows MESSAGE:. They never see the SCRATCHPAD section. Keep the message itself exactly as short and natural as it would otherwise be - the thinking does not belong in it.

Use these two plain labels, each on its own line, every time. Do not use angle brackets, tags, markdown headers or any other formatting for them.
